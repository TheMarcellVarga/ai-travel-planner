# Travel Product Guardrails

## Use When
- Writing product copy.
- Changing pricing or entitlements.
- Adding route recommendation claims.
- Reviewing share pages, booking handoff copy, or AI output language.

## Rules
- Wild Route is a planner and route discovery tool, not a booking engine.
- Do not claim guaranteed prices, availability, safety, weather, visa eligibility, or legal permission.
- Make provider handoffs explicit.
- Keep free/pro limits concrete and server-enforced.
- Preserve the one-promoted-stop free-plan rule unless the roadmap changes.
- Avoid unsafe adventure framing, illegal activity, animal harm, or environmental irresponsibility.

## Checklist
- Is the claim supported by app data or provider data?
- Is freshness visible when relevant?
- Is the booking boundary clear?
- Is paid placement or promotion clear?
- Are safety/legal/visa claims avoided or properly caveated?
- Does copy still sound like Wild Route rather than generic travel SaaS?
